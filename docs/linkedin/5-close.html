04 · Decisions

Small rules that keep the edit honest.

Anchor to words, not secondsIf the word is not in the transcript, the item is skipped with a warning. It never lands in the wrong place.
Preview before XMLSeconds to look, minutes to export. The slow step only runs after approval.
Hand off, do not replacePremiere keeps the craft: grading, music, final polish.
Warn on unknown configA typo in the plan prints a warning instead of being silently ignored.
